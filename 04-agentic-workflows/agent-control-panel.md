**Access Control:**

Juno operates strictly under an On-Behalf-Of (OBO) delegated authentication model. It inherits the exact identity, enterprise roles, and security entitlements of the logged-in user.
- Visibility Boundary: Juno can only see and search what you have explicit permission to access. It does not have elevated tenant-wide, administrative, or super-user visibility across the company.
- Zero Impersonation: Juno cannot bypass ACLs, read other users' private mailboxes, or view confidential documents in restricted spaces you have not been invited to.

Microsoft 365 (outlook): What Juno Can Reach:
- Your personal mailbox, sent items, drafts, and subfolders.
- Your personal calendar, meeting invites, and shared team calendars you have accepted.
- Your Outlook To-Do task lists and flagged items.
What Is Blocked:
- Other employees' private inboxes or personal calendars (unless explicitly shared with you via Exchange permissions).
- Microsoft 365 tenant-wide administration logs, security audit logs, or eDiscovery search vaults.
- Everything else that is not allowed by the "What Juno can reach" section above.

What Juno Can Reach (webex):
- Direct 1:1 message histories in conversations you are a party to.
- Group rooms, spaces, and team channels where you are an active member.
- Messages in public/discoverable spaces within the organization.
What Is Blocked:
- Private spaces or direct messages between other colleagues where you are not a participant.
- Direct access to unshared call recordings or private transcripts of meetings you did not attend.

**Fallback Protocols:**

When an unexpected error or stop condition fires, Juno never halts silently or leaves you with an empty screen. Instead, it follows a strict protocol:
	1.	Preserve partial progress (deliver whatever verified data was already fetched).
	2.	Explicitly state the exact failure boundary (e.g., "Outlook Tasks API timed out; using cached task state from 05:57 AM").
	3.	Hand control back to you with structured, actionable next steps.
